# Literature access notes (Stage 1)

No PDFs or reference directory were present in the workspace at the start of Stage 1. The two requested papers were checked through legal public DOI/publisher/abstract pages on 2026-09-24.

## Navigation of Robotic Swarmalators With Dynamics and Constraints

Xinyue Xu, Wei Xiao, Steven Ceron, IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters 11(2), 1066–1073 (2026), DOI [10.1109/LRA.2025.3640404](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2025.3640404), [IEEE Xplore record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11278087/).

The public abstract supports only the high-level statement that swarmalator space–phase organization is treated as a planner and then executed under omnidirectional, unicycle, or bicycle dynamics and constraints, with CBF-based obstacle handling. IEEE full text was not accessible in this workspace, so the requested pages 2, 3, and 6–8 were not treated as read. No robot parameter was used as traffic calibration, and this project is not a reproduction.

## A cooperative decision-making method for CAVs from the perspective of opinion dynamics

Xuekai Liu, Peng Hang, Yunpeng Wang, Jian Sun, Transportation Research Part C 182, 105412 (2026), DOI [10.1016/j.trc.2025.105412](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trc.2025.105412), [ScienceDirect record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0968090X25004164), [SSRN abstract](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5098114).

Public indexed text/abstract was sufficient to verify the plan–preference–consensus–rolling-execution chain, state-aware utilities, asymmetric influence weights, and shared safety execution assumptions. The publisher page itself was not fully fetchable here and no local PDF was present; this is not a full-text reproduction. The present E baseline is an independent rolling timing controller.

## Direct near-neighbor used for terminology

O'Keeffe, Hong, and Strogatz, “Oscillators that sync and swarm,” [open PMC record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5686229/). This provides the original distinction between spatial position and phase, but its oscillator model is not a vehicle model and its parameters were not copied.
